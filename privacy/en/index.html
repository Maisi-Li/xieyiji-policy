XieYiJi Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Last updated: October 29, 2026

XieYiJi (“the app”) is developed and operated by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We do not operate any server that stores your journal content, and we cannot see your journals.

1. Overview

  • Your journals are stored only on your devices and in your own iCloud account.
  • The app has no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and no account registration.

2. Information we handle

  1. Journal content: journal names, dates, text, Tags, Comments, and the photos and videos you add. Photos and videos are kept unchanged and may include metadata such as capture time and location.
  2. Nickname: used to identify content authors in shared journals. You can skip it and change it at any time.
  3. Subscription status: the app reads from Apple whether you have Premium and when it expires. We never receive your payment information.
  4. Settings and preferences: appearance, text style, prompts, reminder times, and similar settings stay on this device.

3. Where your information is stored

  • On your device: in the app's sandboxed container, which other apps cannot access.
  • iCloud: when iCloud is available, journal content, your nickname, and Premium-related status sync through Apple CloudKit to your iCloud account for use on your other devices. Apple stores and protects this data under the Apple Privacy Policy, and its servers may be located outside your country or region.
  • If iCloud is temporarily unavailable, you can keep writing in journals already on your device. Pending changes stay on the device and upload when iCloud is available again.

4. Shared journals

You can share a journal with others through a private invitation. Once it is shared:

  • Members can see all of that journal's content, including photos, videos, and their metadata.
  • If you post a Comment, members can see the author's nickname.
  • Members with editing permission can edit or delete content.
  • Members can see whether the owner has Premium, because it determines whether members can edit.
  • The owner can remove members or stop sharing at any time, but cannot recall copies that members have already saved or sent elsewhere.

7. Retention and deletion

  • When you delete a whole journal, its data is removed from your iCloud account and this device. After you delete a single photo or video, its file may remain temporarily to support undo and multi-device sync before it is cleaned up.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete journals from iCloud. To delete them completely, delete the journals in the app, or go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage and delete XieYiJi's data.

8. Your rights

Your data stays on your own devices and in your own iCloud account. You can view, edit, or delete your journal content and nickname in the app at any time, turn off iCloud sync, or withdraw system permissions. For any other request about your personal information, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Support email

Email you choose to send us is used only to resolve your issue. The developer handles it personally, keeps it for 30 days after the issue is resolved, then deletes it, and never forwards it to others. You decide whether to send an email and what to include; please do not attach journal text, photos, or videos unrelated to the issue. Sending and receiving email is handled by the email services that you and we use.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 13 (or a higher age required where you live), please use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We will describe significant changes in the app's release notes.
