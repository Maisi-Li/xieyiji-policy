XieYiJi Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

On-device records and preferences

XieYiJi stores journal names, dates, text, Tags, sync state, and upload queues in its on-device app data directory. Original photos and videos are separate files. Appearance, text styles, prompts, nickname, reminders, startup and last-opened journals, and comment-author identity bindings are device-local preferences. Original media may include metadata such as capture time and location.

The current Release interface does not expose an app-owned recording or press-to-speak feature. The operating system controls system-keyboard dictation.

iCloud sync and sharing

When iCloud is available, content and original media sync through Apple CloudKit to your private database or an invited shared database. XieYiJi has no developer-operated content server. You can continue writing in an existing local journal if iCloud is unavailable. Pending sync work remains in the on-device queue. A fresh Release install without a local journal stops at initial discovery if iCloud is unavailable.

Shared journals use private invitations. Members access content according to their permissions; members with editing permission can edit or delete content. Owners can remove members or stop sharing. Original files and their metadata may accompany synced or shared content.

System permissions

Adding media uses the system photo picker. The app asks for permission to add to Photos only when you choose to save original media, and notification permission when you enable reminders. Denying these permissions does not prevent text writing. You can change permissions in system settings.

Support

Contact: lmaxstevens@gmail.com. You choose whether to send an email and what to include. Avoid sending unrelated journal text or original media. Your chosen email service and the recipient's email service handle email delivery.

The developer personally handles support emails and retains them for 30 days after case closure. The developer does not forward support emails.

Retention and deletion

Switch to another journal before deleting a whole journal or leaving a share. Create another owned journal before deleting your last owned journal. After cloud confirmation, the app cleans up the target's on-device data. A failed media-directory deletion keeps a successfully written cleanup marker and retries on later launches. If storage cannot persist the marker or remove files, the app reports incomplete cleanup.

Files for individually deleted media may remain for undo, synchronization, or retained deletion records. Stopping sharing or removing a member cannot remove copies already saved, exported, or sent elsewhere. Delete those copies in their separate destinations. Uninstalling the app does not delete iCloud journals.

Other information

XieYiJi has no ads, tracking SDKs, third-party login, or app-operated account registration. You can skip nickname entry.
